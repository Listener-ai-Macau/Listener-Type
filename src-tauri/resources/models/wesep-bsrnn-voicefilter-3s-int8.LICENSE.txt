This file is an INT8 ONNX conversion of the WeSep BSRNN ECAPA VoxCeleb1
target-speaker extraction checkpoint published by the WeNet community:

  https://github.com/wenet-e2e/wesep
  https://www.modelscope.cn/datasets/wenet/wesep_pretrained_models

The conversion separates the original PyTorch STFT/ISTFT operations from the
neural-network core, quantizes eligible weights, and losslessly splits the
speaker encoder from the separator graph. Listener Type performs the equivalent
STFT/ISTFT locally. No training data is included.

The embedded ECAPA speaker model and target-speaker model were trained on
VoxCeleb. WeSpeaker documents that VoxCeleb pretrained models follow the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license of their corresponding
dataset:

  https://github.com/wenet-e2e/wespeaker/blob/master/docs/pretrained.md
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en
